Frequently Asked Questions

How do I deposit funds?
Go to Dashboard → Add Funds. Select your package and follow the instructions. Send exact amount via USDT (TRC20) to the provided address. Upload transaction ID and screenshot for verification.
How long do withdrawals take?
Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours. Minimum withdrawal is 1,000 PKR. A 2% processing fee applies. Make sure your account is active and you have completed daily ads.
What are the working days?
Monday to Friday: Full functionality including ads and withdrawals. Saturday & Sunday: No new ads, but you can still deposit and check your balance. Support is available on weekends with limited hours.
How are daily rewards calculated?
Daily reward is divided equally among 5 ads. For example, if your daily reward is 500 PKR, each ad gives 100 PKR. Complete all 5 ads to earn your full daily reward. Rewards are credited after admin approval.
Why is my deposit pending?
Deposits are manually verified by admin to ensure transaction accuracy. This usually takes 5-30 minutes. If it's taking longer, check your transaction ID and screenshot, or contact support.
How to change my password?
Go to Profile → Change Password. Enter your current password, then new password (min 6 characters). Confirm and save. You'll be logged out and need to login with new password.
